Our Work

A holistic approach to poverty and neglect.

Shallom International Ministry is a registered Nigerian NGO founded by Rev. Shafa S. Salma on January 29, 2011, in Jos. We work to fulfil the Great Commission and promote peace, education, health, good governance and community empowerment — with no discrimination.

Education

Learning for the neglected

Our centres support the poor and disadvantaged with reading, writing and vocational training — including adult education and mentorship for out-of-school girls.

Leadership & Management

Training exceptional leaders

We believe institutional failure stems from poor leadership. We train, groom and mentor ethical leaders in partnership with professional experts.

Shallomim Peace Desk

Peace and reconciliation

Conflict resolution training, mediation, peace conferences and inter-faith dialogue — building communities that live without fear.

Poverty & Nutrition

Food security first

We train communities, especially women, in sustainable crop growing and balanced, affordable nutrition — because malnutrition harms a child's whole future.
